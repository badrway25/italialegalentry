The design decision that matters here: the sequence marks ONE current step — the first module not yet attended (`aria-current="step"`: its place in the accent's ink) — and only that step carries a control. A screen with a "mark as attended" button on every row invites clicking through the course in ten seconds, which would make the attendance record worthless. One step at a time makes the record mean what it says. The attendance record is drawn as a short document — its title, its reference in ILE Mono, its facts — with its caveat attached, the registers' clause: never a badge, never a seal and never in a colour that could read as an approval (REQ-EDU-05). {% endcomment %} {% block page_title %}{{ course.title }}{% endblock %} {% block meta_description %}{% translate "Votre suivi de formation." %}{% endblock %} {% block portal_header %}
