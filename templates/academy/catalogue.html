{% extends "academy/base_academy.html" %}{% load i18n static %} {% comment %} The catalogue — design V5 (W3-E step 2): one register, its filter, and where the person stands. Deliberately NOT a grid of course cards with a cover image, a rating and a "popular" badge. A course is a row of a register: its title (the way to its page, stretched over the row), the body that delivers it, what it is, and its facts — its domain, its form, its length, its modules and its language. V5.32 lane E: the catalogue is a list of the platform's list toolkit (academy.lists.CATALOGUE, components/listing/): the search and the order in the bar, the domain, the form and the teaching language in the drawer «Filtres», the result count and one chip per criterion once something narrows, «Tout effacer». The domain tabs — eight links that wrapped on two lines — left. The row leads with the course's title, then the body that delivers it on its own line (a learner must always be able to say who is answerable for what they are being taught), the summary, and the course's facts. A course the person already follows carries the chip «déjà inscrit»: a word with its mark, not a colour. Then «Où j'en suis»: the person's follow-up in W1-C's summary strip, the way to «Mes formations». {% endcomment %} {% block page_title %}{% translate "Academy" %}{% endblock %} {% block meta_description %}{% translate "Le catalogue de formation : langue, droits et devoirs, éducation civique, sécurité, culture du travail, formation professionnelle, préparation au départ." %}{% endblock %} {% block portal_header %}
