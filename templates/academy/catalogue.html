{% extends "academy/base_academy.html" %}{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The catalogue — design V5 (W3-E step 2): one register, its filter, and where the person stands. Deliberately NOT a grid of course cards with a cover image, a rating and a "popular" badge. A course is a row of a register: its title (the way to its page, stretched over the row), what it is, and on its line of facts the body that delivers it — named first, and in full: a learner must always be able to say who is answerable for what they are being taught —, its domain, its form, its length, its modules and its language. The domains are the register's filter (`?domaine=`), each with its count: the registers' filter, on its hairline, the chosen one under the tabs' indicator — never a row of pills. A course the person already follows carries the chip «déjà inscrit»: a word with its mark, not a colour. Then «Où j'en suis»: the person's follow-up in W1-C's summary strip, the way to «Mes formations». {% endcomment %} {% block page_title %}{% translate "Academy" %}{% endblock %} {% block meta_description %}{% translate "Le catalogue de formation : langue, droits et devoirs, éducation civique, sécurité, culture du travail, formation professionnelle, préparation au départ." %}{% endblock %} {% block portal_header %}
