THE ROUTE BETWEEN TWO SHORES (DESIGN_SYSTEM_V2.md §8.6). The sequence was a row of eight circles joined by a line that ran to the container edge and stopped. It is now a route: a midnight chapter with the two shores of the project named at its ends — Maroc and Vénétie, which is the project's own name — and the prescribed steps as stations ON the line, drawn by `voyage_route` so a dot always sits on the curve whatever the client's text does to the number of steps. What the drawing does NOT claim: which shore each step belongs to. The locked list is a list of labels; assigning them to a side would be an editorial judgement about a legal pathway, and this template does not make those. The route rises from one named shore to the other, the steps are in their prescribed order along it, and nothing else is asserted. The line draws with the scroll (ScrollTimeline via Motion). With reduced motion it is simply drawn. Below 64rem the SVG is not rendered at all and the stations become a vertical list with a spine: a list works at 320px because it is a list. {% endcomment %}
