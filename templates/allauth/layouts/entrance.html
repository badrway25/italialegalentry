Two panels from 64rem: at the reading edge the STAGE — a night niche behind the page's light wall, with the owner's door stage (arch, photograph, golden key, three steps) on its threshold, the four ways drawn on the floor toward it, and the one help line the entrance keeps; at the other end the FOCUS, which carries the account page's content — title, invitation, fields, one primary action. Below 64rem the stage is a short band above the form: the door and the help line. The stage is drawn here in full, and it is complete without script (the rest, the reduced-motion state, the no-JS state); on a page longer than the screen (the signup) the set — the scene and the help line — stays in sight while the form scrolls. Its paint is static/css/v5/_door.css (the door, as drawn) and apps/accounts/static/accounts/entrance.css (the niche, the floor, the lights); static/js/src/entrance.js makes it answer the person — the e-mail field's focus, a valid address, the password — and says the arrival once. The door stage is also the loader's L1 host (`door-stage sig`, id `varco-door`): on the sign-in's submit its key turns once and holds — static/js/src/loader.js, through the form's data-loader-host, which templates/allauth/elements/form__entrance.html writes on the sign-in's forms. The key is the verbatim key of static/brand/original/key.svg, as an HTML layer so that it turns on the compositor. The stage is decorative (aria-hidden) and direction: ltr in every locale: the door, the key and the ways never mirror. In the source the focus comes first and the stage after it: after the skip link the next stop is in the form, and the help line's address follows the form's action instead of standing between the navigation and the first field. The sheet PLACES the two — the stage at the reading edge, and above the form below 64rem — and holds the stage's place from the first frame, so nothing moves if a browser paints before the stage has been parsed. For the same reason the stage's own parts are written in the order that keeps each one's place known before it arrives: the help line before the scene, the door before the floor that hangs from it. THE FOCUS has one hierarchy on every entrance page (step 2 of the family: the form). The EYEBROW names the place — «Espace réservé», with the key the letterhead's plate gives the same words — then the page's ONE title and its content, which allauth's own templates write through the elements of templates/allauth/elements/ (the fields through `fields.html`, the form through `form__entrance.html`). A page that is not a form — «a message has been sent», a link that has expired, too many requests (templates/429.html) — ends with the WAY BACK to the sign-in, for a person who is not signed in; a form never carries it. When a form comes back with errors the DOCUMENT'S TITLE says so first («Erreur — …»): a person who listens to the page hears it before anything else. A session message, which templates/base.html writes before this section, is placed in the form's column by the sheet. THE PHOTOGRAPH is the home's (static/brand/v5: the same registration as V4's 400 px file, 400 to 1200 px, AVIF and WebP; PROVENANCE.json), so the door keeps its focus from the home to the sign-in on a dense screen. Its `sizes` says the WIDEST the photograph is drawn in each range of windows, never less — told less, the browser takes a file too small and stretches it: the photo box is 320 / 368 of the door's width (`--door-w`, entrance.css), read from the sheet's last rule to its first — the short desktop's clamp(14.5rem, 20vw, 18rem), the desktop's clamp(15.5rem, 22vw, 26rem), each cut at the widths where its middle term meets a bound, then the short band (2.75rem), the band from 48rem (5.5rem) and the phone's (4.875rem). The preload names the AVIF set with the same sizes, character for character, so the one file the picture chooses is the one fetched.
