{% load i18n static cms_nav ile_core %}{% comment %} THE PLATFORM SHELL — design V4 (docs/design-v4/CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md §3.9, DESIGN_SYSTEM_V4.md §8 NavigationRail / ContextBar / BottomBar). Quiet, contextual, task-oriented: the public shell answers "what is this site", this one answers "where am I and how do I get to my work". Three regions, in this order: 1. the RAIL — the reserved area's own navigation on the evolved navy, 64 px collapsed / 248 px open, at the reading edge (the end edge in Arabic); persisted collapse (console.js) 2. the CONTEXT BAR — 56 px of glass, sticky: breadcrumb, then «Site public», the account and the segmented language control; below 64rem it is the top bar (menu, mark, page title, account) 3. below 64rem the BOTTOM BAR — four entries and «Menu», which opens the drawer with the whole rail The case header and the next action are rendered by the page (base_portal.html), not here. Contract, from portal.views._render: `portal_nav` (see portal/partials/rail_nav.html), `breadcrumbs`. The class `portal-bar` on the context bar is the contract three test modules hold the platform chrome to ("the platform chrome, not the public header"). {% endcomment %}
