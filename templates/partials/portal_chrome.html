{% load i18n static cms_nav ile_core entrance %}{% comment %} THE CONSOLE SHELL — design V5 (docs/design-v5/V5_4_EXECUTION_PLAN.md §1.18 D06 «Tre livelli», §3.5 item 3; W3-A). Quiet, contextual, task-oriented: the public shell answers "what is this site", this one answers "where am I and how do I get to my work". From 64rem, two regions: 1. the RAIL — night, at the reading edge (the right one in Arabic), 248 px open / 64 px compact, the choice remembered (static/js/rail-pref.js, console.js). Three levels, by form: the SPACE at its head (the mark on its paper plate, the name, and under it the space the page belongs to — «Espace réservé», «Espace entreprise» — then the way to the account's other space when it has one), the SECTIONS (a word on the text axis) and the PAGES (glyph + word; the current one is «il lume»: its glyph on a lit paper disc). Its FOOT is pinned — the list scrolls under it: the account (the glyph and the word of who it is → the profile), sign-out, the rail's control, the language and «Site public». 2. the CONTEXT BAR — 56 px of glass, sticky: the level of the page (the breadcrumb). Below 64rem the rail is laid flat — the same night, in three pieces (DL54-66 … DL54-71): 1. the TOP BAR is the rail's head: the mark on its paper tile (→ the space's first page), the level of the page as its title, the account. No «≡» here: «Menu» stands in the thumb's zone, once. 2. the BOTTOM BAR is the rail's pages: four entries at most — glyph + word, the current one on its lit disc — and «Menu» at the reading end. A page that declares a phone action bar (`data-console-action`; the case file's own bar today) takes the four entries' place and keeps «Menu» at the same end. 3. the DRAWER holds the whole rail — the other space, the sections and their pages, the foot — in a native from the reading edge, on the overlay system (static/js/src/overlay.js, imported by console.js; W2-A): «Menu» is a link to it (`data-overlay-open`) that the system makes the button of a modal drawer — «Slide» in and out, the one scrim, the trap, Esc, the focus on the page one is on and back to «Menu»; without script it stays a link, the system's stylesheet shows the drawer it points at (`:target`), and the close control — a link too — leads back to the page. The page header and the next action are rendered by the page (base_portal.html), not here. Contract, from every producer of a console page (portal.services.shell_navigation): `portal_nav` (see portal/partials/rail_nav.html), `breadcrumbs`. The class `portal-bar` on the context bar is the contract three test modules hold the platform chrome to ("the platform chrome, not the public header"). The entrance release (W1-D, V5.4 plan §1.9; DL54-07): on the first console page after a successful sign-in the mark of the rail's tile — below 64rem the top bar's — is components/loader.html with `release=True`, and the lit doorway plays once, across the page load, never over painted content; every later page carries the .
