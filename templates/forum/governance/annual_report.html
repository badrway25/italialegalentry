The account of the relation follows. The annual relation — and the page that must not lie about where its numbers come from. Every figure below was ASSEMBLED by `governance_services.build_report_sections` from the edition, its commissions, the proposals that were actually decided, the projects and the monitoring entries. Nothing here is typed by hand and nothing here is computed into an index: a number in this document is a count of rows or a sum of indicators somebody declared, which is the only kind of figure a governance report can still defend six months later. The sections are rendered one by one rather than through a generic table loop, because each one has its own columns and inventing a label for a key would be inventing content. A section the data does not support renders its `.empty` line and says who fills it.
