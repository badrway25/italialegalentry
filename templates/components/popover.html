{% comment %} Popover / menu — the overlay system's anchored panel (design V5, V5.4 plan §1.14; the contract docs/design-v5/QA/v54/w2-a/CONTRACT.md; W2-A). A native `popover`: its trigger opens and closes it, a press outside or Esc closes it, all without script. static/js/src/overlay.js adds the place — on the trigger's inline end, below unless the room is above, 4 px away, arriving by a 4 px drop away from the trigger —, the focus on the first row and back to the trigger, the arrows, and the close when the focus leaves it or a row is chosen. At ≤ 40rem a bottom sheet that stays a menu — no trap, no scroll lock — over the one scrim, which takes the whole press that dismisses it (the page behind is never reached), headed by its `label` in the small secondary ink (`.overlay__caption`, hidden from assistive technology: the group's name already says it); unless `anchored`. Contract: `id` (required); `body` (safe HTML: the rows — a `ul.overlay__list` of `.overlay__item` links or buttons, `li.overlay__sep` between groups) — or `body_template`, a template included with the context and `overlay_part` = "body"; optional `label` (the panel's accessible name, a group's — and the phone sheet's head line); `anchored` (True → stays anchored on a phone).
