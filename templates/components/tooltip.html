{% comment %} Tooltip — an icon-only control and the visible twin of its name (design V5, V5.4 plan §1.14, R-21; the contract docs/design-v5/QA/v54/w2-a/CONTRACT.md; W2-A). The tip is the control's own ::after reading its `aria-label` (static/css/v5/_overlay-panels.css): it waits 400 ms under the pointer and fades in 120 ms, it is there at once under the keyboard's focus, it leaves at 0 ms; the pointer may rest on it (WCAG 1.4.13); Esc and a press put it away (static/js/src/overlay.js). Inside a group (`data-tip-group` on the toolbar) a neighbour shows at once. None on a phone: the name is the control's accessible name. Inside a clipping ancestor (a scrolled list, a table, a drawer's body) `layer` uses the one shared top-layer tip instead. PAGE-ONLY CSS (CONTRACT §1, the owner's V5.8 budget): the control's own ::after tip is static/css/v5/_overlay-panels.css, not main.css — a page that includes this component without `layer` links {% static 'css/dist/core/overlay-panels.css' %} in its extra_head, after main.css. The top-layer tip (`layer`, the product's: the rail's words, «⋮» of a row) is main.css's (v5/overlays.css §6).
