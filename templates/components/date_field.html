{% load i18n ile_core %}{% comment %} THE DATE — day / month / year (V5.12 W2, lane B; DECISIONS §5.8; 21st.dev «Calendar» 34713, its look only: no custom calendar grid). In that order in all four languages; the Arabic field runs right to left with its own words. Progressive by construction: the server renders the NATIVE date input inside the field — with the script off it is the browser's own control, and the form submits what it always did. static/js/src/fieldcheck.js turns it into three segmented inputs (`inputmode="numeric"`, 2 / 2 / 4 digits, the focus moving on when a segment is full and back on Backspace in an empty one) inside a fieldset whose legend is the label, and keeps the native input as the hidden ISO value (YYYY-MM-DD) the form submits. An incomplete or impossible date is said in the server's own words (`data-msg-type`, DateField's «invalid»). Contract: `field` (a BoundField of a DateField or a DateInput), `ui` (the field_ui of form_field.html), optional `help`, `label_lang`. {% endcomment %}
