A threshold is a place: a file in SELECTION stands ON threshold A (OJ2) — the list's current item is then the threshold. Which way the list runs is the component's OWN width (a container query at 56rem), never the template's: the level line 56rem and more: the nodes on one level, each step's glyph and words under its node at the reading start, «Vous êtes ici» on the row that is the current item's alone the card under 56rem (a phone, a narrow column): what is true NOW — the current item's glyph and words, the step's rank (on a threshold, its caption heads the card instead: where that phase sits), «Vous êtes ici» — then the list behind the native disclosure «Où j’en suis», closed, as a spine. Still at load (plan §1.5; D16: «in V5.4 the rail is drawn still at load, with no relay»): the travelled line, the ring and «Vous êtes ici» are painted from the first frame, and nothing moves.
