{% load i18n %}{% comment %} Drawer — the overlay system's side panel (design V5, V5.4 plan §1.14; the contract docs/design-v5/QA/v54/w2-a/CONTRACT.md; W2-A). A native that static/js/src/overlay.js opens: «Slide» from its edge in 320 ms, out in 200 ms, the scrim with it. Modal by default (over the one scrim, the trap in script, aria-modal; Esc, the scrim, «Fermer» or a link inside it close it); `modal=False` is the panel BESIDE the list on desktop (show(): no scrim, the page stays usable; Esc, «Fermer», and Back with `history`) and a modal sheet on a phone. PAGE-ONLY CSS (CONTRACT §1, the owner's V5.8 budget): the drawer itself is main.css's (v5/overlays.css); its head, body and foot — and, beside the list, its phone sheet — are static/css/v5/_overlay-panels.css: the page that includes it links {% static 'css/dist/core/overlay-panels.css' %} in its extra_head, after main.css (tests/frontend/test_overlays.py refuses a page that draws one without it). The console's drawer has its own head (portal_chrome.html) and links nothing. Contract: `id` (required); `title`; `body` (safe HTML) — or `body_template`, a template included with the context and `overlay_part` = "body"; optional `actions` (safe HTML of the foot) — or `actions_template` (included with `overlay_part` = "actions"); `edge` = "start" (navigation, from the reading edge; default the inline end, for a detail); `modal` (False → beside the list); `history` (True → the opening pushes one history entry and Back closes the drawer); `return_id` (where «Fermer» leads without script; default `main-content`).
