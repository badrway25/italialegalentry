{% load i18n ile_core ile_forms %}{% comment %} THE STEPS OF A FORM (V5.15 W3, lane B; DECISIONS §5.12) — the fields of a multi-step form, one fieldset per step, from `{% form_steps form as steps %}` (apps/core/templatetags/ile_forms.py). ALL the fields are in the page: without script the steps are a sequence of fieldsets and the form is exactly the same form; static/js/src/steps.js (fetched by fieldcheck.js where a `[data-steps]` form is) shows one step at a time, draws the progress rail (21st.dev «Wizard Steps» 23576, ported: square tiles, connectors that fill, the current label, the position said once, a panel that slides 22 px in the direction of travel), checks a step's fields before the next one, and opens the first step that came back with an error. The address keeps the step (`?etape=`), so the back button and a reload return to it. Each step's head: the step's glyph in its tile (the icon system's treatment, v5/_glyph.css), «Étape n sur N», its title. When the form asks for a review (`review`), the last step opens on it: what was typed, step by step, each with its «Modifier» (written by the script from the fields themselves; never a password). Round 2: the fields come as rows — a pair the form names (`steps_pairs`) shares a row, `.form-step__pair` — and a step's trailing optional fields may gather under a quiet sub-head (`steps_more`, `.form-step__more`), never reordered. The rails, the review and the navigation are painted by the steps' own sheet (core/steps.css, {% steps_assets %}).
