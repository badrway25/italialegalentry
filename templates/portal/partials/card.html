{% load i18n %}{% comment %} A REGISTER OF THE BRIDGE — design V5 (W3-B step 2; docs/design-v5/V5_4_EXECUTION_PLAN.md §1.15, §3.5 item 4). One of the registers an account that is not a person keeps on its dashboard (templates/portal/dashboard.html, «the bridge»), drawn in the person's grammar and with its rules (apps/portal/static/portal/v5/_dashboard.css): a section flat on the page under its head — the domain's glyph, the word, a state worth saying («1 en retard», «2 non lues») as a chip, the one link to its whole page — then its latest rows as a flat list, each with its date, its kind and its state as a chip (components/badge.html IS the chip). Real rows or one line saying who fills the region and when — never a placeholder, never a figure in large, never a box (the six equal cards with their numerals were V4's). A row's title is a record's own words — a request's subject, a file's name —: isolated in its own direction (dir="auto"). Contract: `card` = portal.services.Card (key, title, icon, available, badge_label/kind, items[CardItem], url, url_label, empty_text, empty_action_label/url). `data-card` is the key the tests read. {% endcomment %}
