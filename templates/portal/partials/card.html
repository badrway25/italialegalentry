{% load i18n %}{% comment %} A REGISTER OF THE BRIDGE — design V5 (W3-B step 2; docs/design-v5/V5_4_EXECUTION_PLAN.md §1.15, §3.5 item 4). One of the registers an account that is not a person keeps on its dashboard (templates/portal/dashboard.html, «the bridge»), drawn in the person's grammar and with its rules (apps/portal/static/portal/v5/_dashboard.css): a section flat on the page under its head — the domain's glyph, the word, a state worth saying («1 en retard», «2 non lues») as a chip, the one link to its whole page — then its latest rows as a flat list, each with its date, its kind and its state. Real rows or one line saying who fills the region and when — never a placeholder, never a figure in large, never a box (the six equal cards with their numerals were V4's). A row's title is a record's own words — a request's subject, a file's name —: isolated in its own direction (dir="auto"). The states are the CHIP's, through their producers (W3-E step 2: components/badge.html, which translated a legacy `kind`, is retired on console pages): a request's member (its producer named by `is_registration`), a scan's member, a date's urgency as a literal register — azione late, attesa close by; the head's late dates azione, its unread count neutro. A communication not yet read says so as the person's dashboard does: the ink dot, the weight, the word «Non lue» — never a chip. A membership's role is a fact of the row's line, not a state. The organisation register draws the account's audience glyph (`card.glyph`: an institution ≠ a university ≠ a company), every other its domain's. Contract: `card` = portal.services.Card (key, title, icon, glyph, available, badge_label, items[CardItem], url, url_label, empty_text, empty_action_label/url). `data-card` is the key the tests read. {% endcomment %}
