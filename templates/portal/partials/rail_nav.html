{% load i18n %}{% comment %} THE RAIL'S LEVELS — design V5 (docs/design-v5/V5_4_EXECUTION_PLAN.md §1.18 D06 «Tre livelli»; W3-A): space → section → page, each by its form, never by decoration. The space's own name is the line under the name at the rail's head (templates/partials/portal_chrome.html); this partial draws the rest, in the rail and in the phone drawer: `part="space"` LEVEL 1, continued — the way to the account's OTHER space when it has one, named as that space names itself («Espace entreprise» from «Espace réservé», and back). Nothing for an account that has one space. `part="foot"` THE FOOT — who the account is (its glyph, its name, its word → the profile), sign-out, the language by its own name and «Site public». `where="rail"`: pinned under the list, with the rail's own control, sign-out a lone glyph whose word console.js shows beside it. `where="drawer"`: the same foot under the drawer's list, where a finger finds no hover — sign-out is a row with its word, and the rail's control is not drawn (nothing folds there). (no `part`) LEVELS 2 and 3 — the sections, a word on the text axis each, and their pages: glyph + word, the glyph on a disc that the current page lights («il lume»). A section whose word would only repeat the space's name or its one entry («Academy») is drawn as a rule, without the word. `id_prefix` ("rail" / "drawer") keeps the ids that label each list distinct between the two copies. Contract: `portal_nav` = portal.services.shell_navigation(…): a list of {key, label, url, current, icon, group, bottom, kind, space}. An entry of kind "space" is the way to another space: level 1 draws it, the list never does (the entries of a kind follow one another).
