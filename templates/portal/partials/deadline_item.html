{% load i18n %}{% comment %} A DATE OF THE PERSON'S — one row of «Mes échéances», design V5 (W3-E; docs/design-v5/V5_4_EXECUTION_PLAN.md §3.5 item 7, §1.2; platform.md §3 «rows with the status chip»). The dashboard's row grammar (`.db-task`, apps/portal/static/portal/v5/ _dashboard.css, on every console page): the words — the date's title as the team wrote it, isolated in its own direction, its kind, its description and its source —, then its state as a chip, then the date. The state, said once, as a chip: a date past due is azione («En retard»: terracotta, the disc with clock hands — red never means lateness), a date close by attesa («Bientôt»: amber, the dashed ring); a closed one says its member through the registry (core.chips «portal.DeadlineStatus»: «Terminée» is a fact, «Annulée» carries nothing). An open date further off has no chip: its date says it, under the section «À venir». The date keeps its glyph — the glyph of its register (F13, W3-C: alarm-clock when late, clock when close by, the calendar otherwise) — in the dashboard's grammar of a date (`.db-due`). Its distance in words («dans 9 jours», «en retard de 3 jours») is the owner's copy (B1 item 4): no live string says it, so the date is drawn alone. Contract: `row` = portal.services.present_deadline(...). {% endcomment %}
