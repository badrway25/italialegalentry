The inbox of the signed-in account (portal.services.inbox): staff-authored, read-only. The only action is «Marquer comme lue» (POST, CSRF-protected, works without JavaScript). The register's grammar: the page's head, then the BAND — the one counter of the unread event, said once, in the page's own sentence (a live region: the page's one) —, then the communications, newest first. A row is the dashboard's row of a communication, whole: the project's mark as its sender (core.icons «ile-mark»: «Équipe du projet, as an actor or a sender» — every communication here is the team's, as the page's line says, and the mark says it on every row), the subject in its own direction, the date; an unread one says so three ways and never by colour — an ink dot, the heavier weight, the word «Non lue» —, and keeps its one act at the row's end. The whole row is the way to the communication; its act stands above that way. No counter on a row: the band holds the count, the rail its entry. The notification centre (a popover from the top bar, a sheet on phones — plan §3.5 item 3) is not built: it needs a view that returns its rows as an asynchronous fragment (W2-A's NOTES, «Handed to»).
