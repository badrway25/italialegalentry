Design V5 (W3-E step 2), in the console's register grammar: the page's head; the requests waiting for the person's decision, each its answerable party, its facts, the decision's form and its clause; then the person's decisions, each with its state as a chip, the way to withdraw it and every consultation underneath. There, a company asked to see a candidate's identity and the PLATFORM'S OWN STAFF decided, from ``/admin/identity-requests``, with two buttons: "Refuser" and "Communiquer l'identité". No consent from the person concerned, no purpose recorded, no duration, no legal basis (previous-platform audit, part 2 §5, private register). Once communicated, the identity appeared to be communicated for ever. Here the decision is the person's, it names a purpose, it lasts a stated number of days, it can be withdrawn in one click without justification, and every consultation is listed underneath — so the person can see not only that they said yes, but exactly who looked, and when. {% endcomment %} {% block extra_head %}{{ block.super }}{% endblock %} {% block page_title %}{% translate "Communications d’identité" %}{% endblock %} {% block meta_description %}{% translate "Les demandes de communication de votre identité, et vos décisions." %}{% endblock %} {% block portal_header %}
