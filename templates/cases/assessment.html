This is the page that answers "why was I told this", and it is the difference between an orientation and an oracle. It reads the SNAPSHOT, never the current rules: an outcome given in October must still explain itself in March, when the rules have moved on without it. Three things are shown, in this order, because that is the order in which a reader builds trust: 1. the outcome itself, in words, never as a decision 2. the RULES that produced it, each named and versioned 3. the SOURCES those rules stand on, numbered, with their precise references An empty source list is stated rather than hidden.
