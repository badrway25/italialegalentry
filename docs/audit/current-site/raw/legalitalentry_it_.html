New Module

Short-Stay Visas

Uniform Schengen Type C Visa, for a maximum stay of 90 days in any 180-day period. The platform prepares your file and identifies missing items — decision rests exclusively with the consular authority.

01

Tourism

Itinerary, accommodation, reservations, date consistency, and proof of return.

02

Family Visit

Relationship with host, invitation letter, accommodation, and financial guarantee if applicable.

03

Business

Inviting company, mission purpose, meetings or trade fairs, duration, and funding.

04

Invitation

Identification of the genuine purpose and orientation toward the proper pathway.

What this module never does

No outcome forecasts, no booking of consular appointments, no circumvention methods. A prior refusal never implies an automatic rejection: it calls for an individualised analysis.

Before Departure

Prepare Before
Departure

Arriving prepared changes everything: language, workplace codes, rights and duties, safety protocols. Tracks are assigned by profile, sector, or cohort, and can be co-designed with enterprises.

Important Distinction

Courses provided by Italy Legal Entry and its partners are orientation and preparatory training. They are clearly distinguished from vocational and civic-linguistic training programs approved under Article 23 of D.Lgs. 286/1998. A platform certificate is never a ministerial approval.

Transparency

What the Platform Does —
and What It Does Not

ETICA & LEGALITÀ Massima trasparenza verso i cittadini e rispetto rigoroso delle normative vigenti

What We Do

  • Explain procedures clearly in your language
  • Prepare your file and flag missing documents
  • Organize your documents and key deadlines
  • Provide structured pre-departure training
  • Connect you with employers, with your consent
  • Direct you to the competent official authority

What We Do Not Do

  • Issue visas, residence permits, or equivalencies
  • Promise outcomes, expedited timelines, or consular slots
  • Replace an individualised legal consultation
  • Transfer your personal data without your explicit consent
  • Intervene with public authorities on your behalf
  • Accept inaccurate documents or fraudulent declarations
Territorial Network

An Italy – Morocco Axis

The platform relies on a network of desks, professionals, and authorized partners on both sides of the Mediterranean. Each file is assigned based on language, territory, and expertise.
