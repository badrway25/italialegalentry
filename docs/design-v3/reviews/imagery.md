## Violazioni dei vincoli non negoziabili

1. PROGRESSIVE ENHANCEMENT (Django server-rendered + progressive enhancement, non-negotiable) — §4.5 line `.plate__shutter{position:absolute;inset:0;background:var(--min-1);transform:translateY(0)}` plus `.plate[data-revealed] .plate__shutter{display:none}`. The shutter is opaque at rest and is removed only by JS setting `data-revealed`. With JS off or failed, every photograph on the site is hidden behind a solid panel. The project already has the correct pattern — static/css/v2/hero.css:473 and voyage.css:235 both write `html.js [data-reveal]:not(.is-in)` so the no-JS state is the final state — and base.html:2 ships `class="no-js"` with static/js/head.js swapping it to `js`. The spec ignores its own codebase's solution. §6 item (5) even says to TEST 'reduced-motion + no-JS insieme: la pagina dev'essere già lo stato finale', while the CSS it ships guarantees the opposite.

2. MOTION_SYSTEM §4 'Un solo oggetto vivo per pagina' — 'Una pagina IMMERSIVE può avere un loop ambientale ... e UN oggetto indicizzato allo scroll.' §5 M-IMG-2 adds a scroll-linked drift to `.plate--chapter`, which §4.6 places in `pages/home/_pilot.html`. The home ALREADY has two scroll-indexed objects: static/js/src/home.js:95 `scroll(animate(photo, {transform:[...]}), {target: hero, ...})` (the doorway parallax, documented at home.js:15 as 'photograph on scroll DEPTH 18px scroll-linked') and home.js:116 `scroll(animate(line, {strokeDashoffset:[1,0]}), {target: voyage, ...})` (the route line). M-IMG-2 makes three. The spec's defence — 'Non consuma l'unico loop di pagina: è legata allo scroll, non temporale' — answers the loop budget and silently skips the scroll-indexed budget, which is the one it breaks.

3. NO BOX-SHADOW ANIMATION (performance non-negotiable) — §4.5 'Stati': '*hover* (solo `hover:hover`, solo `.plate--route` dentro `<a>`) immagine `scale(1.02)` e cornice a `--hairline-strong`, 180 ms'. The frame IS a box-shadow (`.plate__frame{...box-shadow:inset 0 0 0 1px var(--plate-frame)}`), so 'cornice a --hairline-strong, 180 ms' is an animated box-shadow. §5 M-IMG-5 asserts the exact opposite in the same document: 'cornice `box-shadow` **statica di ruolo**, mai animata ... Nessuna animazione di `box-shadow`'. The implementer is handed two contradictory instructions and one of them breaks the constraint.

4. DESIGN_SYSTEM_V2 §6 (binding): 'Altre fotografie solo su IMMERSIVE e RICH'. §7 classifies news as STANDARD ('informative, FAQ, glossario, news'). §4.3 of the spec assigns `.plate--news` (16:9) to `news/detail` and to list cards, and `.plate--band` to `le-projet` — templates/pages/project.html, an informative page, i.e. STANDARD. §4.3 also assigns `.plate--seal` with `-notte` grading to 'accesso riservato (login/registrazione)', a surface §7 does not list as IMMERSIVE or RICH at all, so it gets both a photograph and a midnight surface with no declared regime. The irony is exact: §4.3 is the section that proposes making the regime machine-verifiable by reactivating `data-mode`, and it is the section that breaks it.

5. WCAG 2.2 AA CONTRAST — §4.5 'Contrasti verificati sullo scrim, caso peggiore' gives `--on-dark` 8.85:1 and `--on-dark-2` 5.52:1. I reproduced those exactly, but only for the terminal stop: `--plate-scrim` is applied as `linear-gradient(to bottom,transparent 38%,var(--plate-scrim) 100%)`, so 82 % opacity exists on one pixel row. Computed along the run over a white frame: at 75 % the composite is rgb(74,84,90) and `--on-dark-2` gives 4.29:1; at 68 % rgb(91,100,105) gives 3.35:1; at 60 % 2.56:1. The chapter legend is five lines (folio, place/date, sentence, credit, archetype note) and therefore crosses that range. The folio/place/credit lines are `--t-folio` (13px mono), which needs 4.5:1. The proof does not cover the case the design creates.

6. IMAGES ≤ 120 KB PER VIEWPORT — §7's census ('Nessun viewport supera 120 KB') is computed at DPR 1 only. The srcset ladder is '480/800/1200/1600/2000 px' and the new per-derivative cap is stated as '140 KB AVIF / 110 KB WebP **fino a 1600 px**' — the 2000px derivative is left uncapped. At 1440 CSS px with DPR 2 the browser selects 2000w for the full-bleed 21:9 chapter, so the one number the constraint actually governs is never computed for the display most reviewers will use.

## Lacune (ciò che l'implementatore dovrebbe inventare)

1. TOKEN NAMES DO NOT MATCH THE CSS. §4.5 declares `--plate-ar-chapter:21/9 · --route:4/5 · --band:3/2 · --seal:4/3 · --news:16/9`, then the stylesheet uses `var(--plate-ar-route)`, `var(--plate-ar-band)`, `var(--plate-ar-seal)`, `var(--plate-ar-news)`. Four of five aspect-ratios resolve to nothing and those frames collapse. Worse, `--route: 4/5` collides with the existing `--route-1..4` colour tokens in static/css/v2/tokens.css — a token named `--route` next to `--route-1: #0f6a72` is a design-system trap.

2. `.plate__img{block-size:100%}` cannot work as written. The image is inside `<picture>`, which is `display:inline` with auto height, so a percentage block-size is indefinite and falls back to auto; `object-fit:cover` then has nothing to fit and the crop discipline of §4.1 rule (2) never happens. The spec never styles `picture` (verified: the string `picture{` appears nowhere in the file). The implementer must invent `.plate__frame picture{display:block;block-size:100%}` or absolute-position the img.

3. `data-priority` is never emitted. `.plate:not([data-priority]) .plate__frame{content-visibility:auto;contain-intrinsic-size:auto 420px}` is the only occurrence in the document; §4.4's markup uses `{% if priority %}` for `loading`/`fetchpriority` but puts no attribute on the `<figure>`. As written, content-visibility:auto applies to the LCP plate too — the opposite of §7's stated intent.

4. THE CHAPTER LEGEND HAS NO POSITIONING. §4.4 prose says 'Sul capitolo è un blocco d'angolo dentro la banda, sopra lo scrim' — but the `<figcaption>` is a sibling of `.plate__frame`, outside the overflow:hidden box, and no rule gives `.plate` position:relative or the legend position:absolute. The only `.plate--chapter .plate__legend` rule in the file is `position:static` inside the forced-colors block, which overrides something that was never written. §6's test ('il cartellino del capitolo non copre mai più del 40 % della banda') presupposes an overlay that does not exist in the CSS.

5. `.plate-strip` IS NOT SPECIFIED. It appears exactly once, as a table cell in §4.3. There is no markup, no CSS, no anatomy — yet §3 credits it with scroll-snap, a native `<dialog>` lightbox, arrows, `aria-current` and a scrollend live-region; §6 adds ≥44px touch targets, ←/→/Home/End, focus return on dialog close; §7 gives it a byte budget; M-IMG-4 animates it. One of six slots, and the most complex component in the document, is a name.

6. §4.4's markup self-contradicts §4.4's prose two lines later: 'Il cartellino sta **sotto** la lastra, mai sopra il soggetto (è il difetto di 21st #2)' immediately followed by 'Sul capitolo è un blocco d'angolo dentro la banda, sopra lo scrim.' Which is it?

TWO INCOMPATIBLE `Photo` DATACLASSES ARE TREATED AS ONE. `pages.services.Photo` (apps/pages/services.py:157) has `srcset`, `share_path`, `place`, `date`, `caption`, `credit` and NO `sizes`. `ecosystem.services.Photo` (apps/ecosystem/services.py) has `webp_srcset`, `sizes`, `share_image` and NO place/date/caption/credit. §4.4's contract demands `srcset` AND `sizes` AND place/date/caption/credit on one object. §4.7 says only that `services.Photo` 'guadagna `avifset`, `focus`, `kind`' — it never says to unify the two shapes, rename `webp_srcset`, or add `sizes` to the pages one. `{{ photo.sizes }}` will render empty for every `pages` caller, silently defaulting srcset to 100vw and destroying the per-viewport byte census in §7.

## Segnali di «cheap / generico / default»

1. M-IMG-3 IS THE FADE-IN-UP IT MOCKS. 'opacity 0→1, translateY(8→0), 240 ms, --ease-out, delay 60 ms' is the single most common AI/template entrance animation in existence. The spec's own performance note pre-empts the objection — 'non un `fade-in-up` generico' — which is a rename, not a design. The owner will not see the naming; he will see a caption fading up, which is exactly what he has already rejected as not-dynamic-enough. It also stacks on top of shell.js's existing global reveal, so the legend fades up twice.

2. THE SHUTTER WIPE IS THE 2026 DEFAULT. A solid panel sliding off a photograph while the image relaxes from `scale(1.03)` to `scale(1)` is the stock Framer/Motion image reveal shipped by every template marketplace. Nothing in M-IMG-1 is specific to this project, this brand or this subject matter. It is competent and it is anonymous — and 'anonymous' is precisely the charge the owner has laid against V2.

3. 24 px OF SCROLL PARALLAX. M-IMG-2's drift is the most-copied cheap-premium tell on the web. At 24 px over a 21:9 band it will be barely perceptible on desktop and invisible at 390 px, so it buys none of the 'dynamic' the owner asked for while spending a budget line (see the scroll-indexed violation) and adding a compositor layer. It reads as a checkbox ticked, not a decision made.

4. hover `scale(1.02)` ON A CARD IMAGE. §4.5 and M-IMG-5. This is the default gesture of every component library on 21st.dev, including several the spec itself rejects. It is also the one state a mouse-owning reviewer will test first.

5. THE FOLIO NUMBER IS COSTUME BY THE SPEC'S OWN STANDARD. §3 rejects candidate #1's '35MM · ISO 400' with the right argument — 'su una piattaforma legale è costume, non credibilità' — and then §4.4 introduces `.plate__folio` in uppercase mono with `--track-folio`. Unless the folio is a real register number that resolves to a real archive entry, it is the same affectation with a different typeface. Nothing in the spec says what a folio IS or who assigns it.

6. A FULL-BLEED 21:9 BAND WITH A BOTTOM GRADIENT SCRIM AND LIGHT TEXT is the most generic premium hero pattern on the internet — and it is what `.plate--chapter` is. The 'lastra' concept earns its distinction from the cartellino, the 2px radius and the refusal of shadow; the chapter slot throws that away and reverts to the default. It will read as a template hero with a caption bolted on.

7. FIVE RATIOS UNDERCUT THE ARGUMENT. §4.1 rule (2) is 'il taglio è deciso, non indovinato' — then §4.3 ships 21:9, 4:5, 3:2, 4:3 and 16:9, which is essentially every ratio in use. A house that had really decided its crop would ship two or three. As it stands the ratio table reads as coverage, not conviction.

8. THE HOVER/FOCUS SPLIT IS A TELL. §4.5 gives hover a scale and a frame change, and focus only 'anello 2 px --accent-ring + alone 3 px al 30 % sul link'. Functionally defensible, but it means a keyboard reviewer sees a default focus ring where a mouse reviewer sees a designed gesture — the classic signature of a design that was built mouse-first and retrofitted.
