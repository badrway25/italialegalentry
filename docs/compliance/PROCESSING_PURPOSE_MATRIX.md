# Processing-purpose matrix (V5.16 §4)

- Written 28 October 2026 by lane P2 (label `v516-p2-build`), from the lane P audit
  ([`PRIVACY_PROCESSING_AUDIT.md`](PRIVACY_PROCESSING_AUDIT.md), rows P1–P19) and the code on branch `v516-p2`.
- Directive: V5.16 §4 «Do not assume Article 6(1)(b) for everything … If the lawful basis is not established from
  approved legal/governance material: mark: LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED. Do not invent it.»; §11 for retention.
- **Approved material** used for a basis: **ANX** = the legal lead's French courtesy translation of Allegato 1,
  privacy section §1–§8 (14 Sept 2026). The Italian Allegato 1, which prevails, is not in hand. So every basis taken
  from ANX is also subject to that text, and the column says so with «(ANX §n)».
  - ANX §3 names: art. 6(1)(b) for answering orientation and information requests and for «gérer les parcours
    informatifs de la plateforme», and on the same basis for the companies' expressions of interest and event
    registrations; 6(1)(c) for the controller's legal obligations; 6(1)(f) for site security and abuse prevention;
    6(1)(a) for the optional periodic communications (newsletter).
  - ANX names no art. 9 condition. ANX §2 only asks users not to put special-category data in free fields.
  - ANX §5 says that data are not communicated to third parties or put in relation with each other **without the
    express consent** of the data subject.
- Nothing here is legal advice and nothing here is a decision. Every basis is a **candidate** until the legal review
  confirms it. A cell that says LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED is an open question, not a gap the team may fill.

## Retention classes (V5.16 §11)

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CONFIGURED** | the code or the runbook sets it; `file:line` given |
| **APPROVED POLICY** | an approved document states it; source given |
| **PROPOSED** | a project document proposes it; nobody approved it |
| **UNRESOLVED** | nothing sets or states it. It must be resolved before production (§11) |

## What the matrix decides, and what it does not

- **6(1)(b) is not used by default.** It appears only where ANX states it: orientation and information requests,
  companies' expressions of interest, event registrations (M-06, M-15, M-16). It also appears as a «candidate» where
  a person asks for a service (M-05, M-10's request). It is **not** given to security logs, fraud prevention, the
  audit trail, legal compliance, internal helpdesk notes, Academy plans or the selection workflow (§4).
- **Retention to resolve before production** (§11): M-01 journald, M-03, M-04, M-05 inactivity, M-07 to M-12,
  M-14, M-16, M-18, M-19, M-20 off-server. These map to OF-04 to OF-07 in the privacy notice.
- **Owner/legal inputs this matrix needs**: the LIA for M-01 to M-03; the named legal obligation, if any, for M-04;
  OF-10 (the platform's basis); the art. 9 position ([`SPECIAL_CATEGORY_AUDIT.md`](SPECIAL_CATEGORY_AUDIT.md)); the
  retention policy per purpose.
