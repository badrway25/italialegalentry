## 5. SIGNATURE COMPONENT — «La soglia» (journey rail), in one paragraph

Seven locked steps (`JOURNEY_STEPS`: situation & objective → pre-assessment & possible routes → preparation & training before departure → procedures with competent authorities → departure preparation → arrival & first steps → support & inclusion); Morocco side then Italy side; between them the owner's doorway arch, drawn in thin navy line with the key at rest, is the frontier the rail passes through. Travelled segment in accent, drawn once at first paint up to the current step (static and complete under reduced motion); current step ringed, «Vous êtes ici» in words; no percentage, no plane, no flags, no luggage, no passports. Dashboard = horizontal; case file = vertical with the date each step was reached; public parcours = vertical spine with a scroll-linked beam and one editorial photograph per step. The brief's narrative sequence (Morocco → training → documents → authorised actor → procedure → Italy → post-entry) maps onto these seven domain-locked steps: "documents" and "authorised actor" are the `detail` / `actor` fields of steps 2–4, not new steps.
