# ICON LICENSES — the ILE sprite (design V5)

Italy Legal Entry™ · W1-B, the icon system (V5.4 plan §1.7, §2.3; the research map `ICON_SEMANTIC_MAP.md` Q5) ·
8 October 2026. The product ships **two sprites** (since V5.12 W2: `static/img/icons.svg`, the chrome, and `static/img/icons-domain.svg`, the domain concepts — the registry's `SPRITE_OF`), generated by `scripts/build_icons.py` from
one source per symbol in `static/icons/src/<id>.svg` and named by the registry `apps/core/icons.py`. No icon library
is installed or loaded (no `lucide`, `phosphor`, `heroicons`, `tabler`, `@radix-ui/react-icons` — in `package.json`,
the scripts or the templates): the geometry below was **copied into the sources at build time of the system**, from
the vendored package `lucide-static 1.48.0` kept outside the repository
(`C:/tmp/ile_v5_council/proof/.vendor/package/`, read-only), and nothing is fetched at runtime.

## 1. What is whose

| Origin | Count | What |

| V4 aliases (`home`, `list`, `globe`) | 3 | each draws the geometry of its V5 symbol above (`house`, `send`, `languages`); they leave with their last producer — `user` and `key` left on 9 October 2026 |

The ILE drawings are part of the product and follow the repository's licence (`package.json`: UNLICENSED — all
rights reserved to the project). The donors keep their notices (§3), which this file carries for the product.

**The ILE treatment** (one hand, plan §1.7): the donor's root attributes (`stroke-width="2"`, round caps and joins)
are never copied — the sprite's symbols have no width, cap or join of their own; the `.icon` rule draws every
symbol at 1.5 px, square caps, miter joins, `currentColor`, through `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke`. Where the
map says so, a 12 % tone sub-path is added (`fill="currentColor" fill-opacity=".12" stroke="none"`); a donor's
stroked r = 1 dot becomes the ILE square dot (the three points of `ellipsis-vertical` are 1-unit squares, so that the
1.5 px stroke makes a solid dot at every size); `alarm-clock` loses its two feet. Every source declares its origin in
its one comment (`Lucide "<name>" 1.48.0 (ISC)` or `ILE drawing`), and `scripts/build_icons.py` refuses a
source whose comment disagrees with the registry.

## 4. How a donor enters, and how one leaves

- A new glyph is a request to the icon map, not an import: it is drawn as an ILE drawing, or its geometry is copied
  from the same vendored `lucide-static 1.48.0` into `static/icons/src/<id>.svg` with the origin comment,
  registered in `apps/core/icons.py` (tier, floor, donor, meaning, group) and listed here. Two libraries never mix
  (the map's rule 2).
- A symbol leaves by deleting its source and its registry entry; the build fails on any orphan either way, and
  `tests/frontend/test_icons.py` keeps this file, the registry and the sprite in agreement.
- 28 Oct 2026 (V5.12 W1, lane B): `wrench` left (its one seat, the company rail's «Mes besoins», read «repair»);
  `briefcase-business` entered from the same package, with the 12 % tone on the case and the ILE square dot.
- 28 Oct 2026 (V5.12 W2, lane B, DECISIONS §4 «Symbols to vendor»): twelve donors entered from the same package —
  `presentation`, `list-checks`, `file-search`, `book-text`, `earth`, `user-cog`, `messages-square`,
  `message-square-text`, `files`, `shield-check`, `mic`, `mail-plus` (it exists in 1.48.0). None is Feather-derived
  (the package's LICENSE list), so the ISC notice alone covers them. The 12 % tone is added on the board, the page,
  the cover, the bubbles, the front sheet, the shield and the capsule; `list-checks`, `earth`, `user-cog` and
  `mail-plus` stay monoline. `list-checks` and `shield-check` draw a check: both are in `CHECK_BEARING` with their
  only seats. The sprite is split in two the same day; the chrome file fell from 30 878 B to about 17 KB.
- 29 Oct 2026 (V5.19, lane v519-k, the owner's confirmed contact channels): `message-circle` entered from the same
  package (ISC, not Feather-derived), with the 12 % tone in the bubble — the WhatsApp channel's glyph, beside its word
  «WhatsApp». No WhatsApp brand mark is drawn: Lucide has none, and Meta's WhatsApp brand guidelines ask that its
  glyph be used unaltered (its own shape and colours), which the ILE treatment (one 1.5 px stroke in `currentColor`)
  would alter; a CC0 copy (Simple Icons) carries no trademark licence. The channel is named by its word instead.
- 30 Oct 2026 (V5.22, SECTION_SYSTEM_V2 §4.9 and §3, lane S): seven donors entered from the same package for the V2
  sections' stations, seats and tiles — `layers`, `hand-helping`, `calendar-sync`, `waypoints`, `map-pinned`, `landmark`,
  `file-badge` (none is Feather-derived: the ISC notice alone covers them; `layers`, `map-pinned` and `landmark` take the
  12 % tone). They live in a third, page-only sprite, `static/img/icons-marks.svg` (≤ 16 KiB), with the T2-size copies of
  the thirteen ILE marks — `icons.svg` and `icons-domain.svg` stay untouched. The marks are ILE drawings, the project's
  own (no donor): drawn once on the 48-unit grid in `static/icons/marks/<id>.svg`, built by `scripts/build_icons.py` into
  their inline T1 partials (`templates/components/sezioni/marks/`) and their sprite copies.
