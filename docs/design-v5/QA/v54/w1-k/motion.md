## Motion, read from frames (S2 and S3 in this chair's harness; S1 not opened)

- **S2** (fr, 1440). Captured with CDP `Animation.setPlaybackRate` 0.25 plus screenshots, and with a 1× screencast.
  - Arrival: one gold streak runs the arch once in 900 ms. It changes ≤ 0.07 % of the stage (0.12 % frame to frame),
    so at 1× it is not seen.
  - Submit: the press colour lands at 0 ms; the busy key shows from ≈ 150 ms. The stage key makes two half-turns
    through its mirror image at ≈ 0.1–0.75 s and ends on the verbatim key. The frame light runs at ≈ 0.8–1.35 s and
    the thread starts at 0.5 s. Together they change ≤ 0.23 % of the stage.
  - Reduced motion: the stage stays still and the button still says it is busy.
  - States: hover darkens the primary within 60 ms; the focus ring is instant; nothing loops.
- **S3** (fr). Held frames via `?t=` every 100 ms at 1440 and every 80 ms at 390 / DPR 2, plus `?play=1` at 1× and
  0.25×.
  - Clock: one clock drives 37 / 23 WAAPI animations, ending at 3 269 / 1 964 ms.
  - Change against REST at 1440: 3.0–3.5 % up to 1.1 s; 1.8–2.8 % while the lights travel; 5.8–10.6 % while the door
    answers (2.0–2.9 s). The answer is released in ≈ 200 ms, and the frame equals REST at 3.3 s (0.0 %). A 1.47 %
    baseline in these figures is text antialiasing on composited layers; the glyphs are identical.
  - Phones: 3.3–3.9 %, with a 7.9 % peak.
  - Under the hand: 1.16 % of the screen changes, and 0.0 % remains after leave.
  - The square halos on the light heads appear on the shared held frame and in the 0.25× DPR 2 playback
    (`motion/S3/cmp-square-halo-*.png`).

## How I read the anchors (five lines)

1. A 4 is a comparison, not a tally. The question is whether the surface would sit beside the plan's references
   (Linear, Stripe, Mercury, e-Residency, GOV.UK) without a caveat. A caveat on its costly element or on its one
   effect keeps it at 3.
2. Premium is TS §21.1 Q1–Q8. Any «No» means 2 or below. No «No» with several «Partly» means 3. A 4 needs «Yes» on
   Q1, Q2 and Q8: the expensive thing, the custom thing and the one effect must each survive a 2× crop and a frame
   series.
3. Coherence is Q3, measured first (harness on S2 and S3; curve fit and edge profiles on the S1 stills) and then
   judged by eye across screens. A minor measured miss, such as three 2 px gaps, does not cost the point. A
   systematic one does: a component off the 4 px scale, a second page-head system, shadows past the cap.
4. Motion is judged on frames, never on intent. A change under ≈ 1 % of the screen is not seen and earns nothing. A
   seen effect costs premium when it takes its answer back, shows artefacts or renders light as haze.
5. 3 means well made but nothing a template would not also give, or ambitious with visible craft faults. 2 means the
   premium ingredients are absent; 1 means broken; 5 means it would raise the reference set.
