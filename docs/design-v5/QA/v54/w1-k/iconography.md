## How I read the anchors (five lines, for later chairs of this lens)

1. 4 is a comparison, not a tally: the still must hold beside the family's named references at 1440 and 390 with
   nothing to excuse, and «better than before» is not evidence (TS §2.1).
2. Premium: the instrument sets the ceiling. Any «No» on Q1–Q8 caps the score at 3; two or more «No», or a §19 F, give 2;
   a broken surface gets 1. 4 needs no «No», at most two «Partly» (the taste review's tolerance, TS §21.3 item 4) and the
   swap test passing on every still; 5 adds a costly thing a reference would envy.
3. Coherence: measure first, on the surface's own token list (the probe's union for pre-V5.4 work; «Lo spigolo» folds
   are noted, not counted), then look. Every threshold met and one mark per meaning opens 4; one miss gives 3; two misses,
   or a state drawn in two languages on one screen, give 2; no system gives 1.
4. Marks count under both scales. A glyph with two concepts, a concept with two glyphs, or a signature (the key, the
   steps, the gold) spent on a label or a header mark is a coherence breach and at least a «Partly» on premium Q2 and Q3.
5. Name the still under every number. Where a surface is stills-only, estimate from ×8 crops and state the error, and
   cite corpus measures as corpus, never as your own.
