  laid over a picture, not as a lit object. That is the main reason Q1 stays at «Partly» on both.

## How I read the anchors (for the next chairs of this lens)

1. **4** is not «good». It means the surface can hang beside its family's named references (§3.1 item 5) without reading
   as the weaker one. Here those are GOV.UK, Mercury and Linear; e-Residency, Mercury and Wise; Collège de France,
   e-Residency and the Rijksmuseum. On the premium review that takes no «No», a «Yes» on Q1 and at most two «Partly».
2. **3** is a page that works, offends nothing and buys nothing: no «No», with «Partly» the majority. **3.5** when at least
   half the answers are a clean «Yes».
3. One «No» on Q1 (nothing costly) or on Q3 (measured incoherence) takes a surface below 3. Two take it to 2.5 at best.
4. Coherence is measured first and seen second. Every criterion met is the floor of 4, and each measured miss costs half a
   point (one → 3.5, two → 3). What the eye or the corpus adds — a register that changes between screens, a second hand, a
   second accent — costs at most another half point.
5. From the image seat, Q1 cannot be «Yes» while any of these holds, whatever the composition earns:
   - a photograph that must be read is drawn below IM-10's size;
   - a row of photographs sits on two white points;
   - a picture fails IM-1's fit question.
