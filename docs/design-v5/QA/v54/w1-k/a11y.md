## Evidence

### S1: stills only (by brief), at 390 × 844 and 360 × 800 fr and 390 × 844 ar, four screens

- **What holds.**
  - Nothing overflows horizontally at either width (INDEX health record), and every screen is one column.
  - The dashboard's first screen answers LA-9 on the phone: «Brouillon» since 21.09.2026, «Compléter le
    questionnaire d'orientation», «La personne», «Continuer».
  - The documents screen shows, in its first view, the summary, the filter chips (44 px, a horizontal scroller cut at
    the edge by design), a 44 px search field and the first row.
  - State is carried by words and marks (solid or dashed bars, a hatched progress bar), never by colour alone.
  - The Arabic pages mirror whole, with each tab label on one line.
- **What is below the bar.**
  - **These are desktop cards stacked, not a phone composition.** The dashboard's full page is 6 058 device px, which
    is 3 029 px or 3.6 screens. It ends on three empty-state cards (Mes échéances, Communications, Academy)
    (`S1-dashboard-fr-390x844-full.png`).
  - **Targets.** Secondary buttons are 36 px tall, measured on the stills as 72 device px: Profil, Sécurité du compte,
    Remplacer la pièce, Consulter. That is under RE-3's 44 px and over the 24 px of WCAG 2.5.8.
  - **Navigation.**
    - Five tabs carry 11 px labels, three of them on two lines and hyphenated by rule
      (`apps/portal/static/portal/console.css` l.497–532: «Communica-tions», «Tableau de / bord», «Mes / documents»).
    - The header carries a second menu (the hamburger).
    - The journey and request pages light no tab.
  - **Phone details.**
    - The «non analysé» chips are stretched to 296 px, so they read as empty fields (dashboard, «Derniers dépôts»).
    - Separators are orphaned at line ends («3 pièces demandées · 0 sur 3 validées ·», at both widths).
    - A version string is broken inside its token («2026- / 09-17.1», in the journey's notice).
    - The journey and request pages each have two left gutters on one screen: 36 px for the header block, 16 px for
      the content.
  - **First screens spent on air or disclaimers.** `S1-request-fr-390x844.png` shows three facts.
    `S1-journey-fr-390x844.png` shows the informative notice and no result.
  - **RTL.**

    - On `S1-dashboard-ar-390x844.png` the persona eyebrow sits off the right margin.

## How I read the anchors (five lines, for the next chairs of this lens)

1. **4 on Mobile** is a phone experience I would set beside the references' phone pages without apology. It is composed
   for the phone at every size of the set, 375 × 667 and 844 × 390 included, not a desktop page that survives on one.
2. **The floor comes first**, WCAG 2.2 AA on a phone: no overflow from 320, reflow at 320, 1.4.12 without loss, targets
   of at least 24 px (2.5.8), focus visible and never obscured, both orientations, RTL mirrored with the mark never
   mirrored. A floor failure caps the score at 3.
3. **The project's phone rules decide between 3 and 4**: RE-1 (the phone's own first screen), RE-3 (44 px under a coarse
   pointer), RE-4 (reading text and inputs of at least 16 px). Missing one across a page keeps a surface below 4,
   however good the picture.
4. **3** = it works at every size, but the phone gets an adaptation (a stack, a band) that empties or breaks at some
   size of the set. **3.5** = a composition made for the phone that holds at every size, with misses an excellent
   product would not ship. A half point sits only between anchors and never rounds up to a pass.
5. **Say what the set did not show.** S1 was two of five sizes, in stills only, so its 3 is a partial reading. Everything
   here is emulated Chromium, and anything inferred, such as the iOS zoom, is named as inferred.
