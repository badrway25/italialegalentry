## How I read the anchors (for the next chairs of this lens)

1. **4** means I could set the still beside the named references (Collège de France, e-Residency, Mercury, Linear) and see no step down in craft. Premium also needs no «No» on Q1–Q8 and coherence every Q3 measure met. «No No» is necessary, never sufficient.
2. **3** means competent work a careful team ships, whose best idea is real but whose execution shows its seams: forced display spacing, a small-text load, a flat or static stage. It is «Partly» where a 4 would say «Yes».
3. **2** means a «No» on a structural question (expensive · custom · coherent) that a user feels from screen to screen, such as the h1 changing voice room to room. **1** means no system at all, or an F on the eighteen.
4. Through type, I look for one voice per surface, three weights that signal, sizes that contrast (1 px steps are noise), a display set as display (the word space follows the tracking) and Arabic set, not mirrored. A type miss weighs on coherence first, and on premium once it shows at first glance.
5. I measure coherence before I feel it. I run the probe, then read every off-scale gap and every shadow by name: space-between distributions and drawn figures are not spacing, and drawing rings are counted first, then argued. One clean number never carries a page my eye finds split.
