## 5. How I read the anchors — for the next chairs of this lens

1. 4 is the whole surface «credible alongside excellent international digital products»: one region at the bar beside parts merely well made is 3.5 (Hd §8.3's H-E line), and a signature alone never lifts premium to 4.
2. Premium reaches 4 only with no «No» on TS §21.1 and Q1 at «Yes» — the costly thing done right, and nothing an excellent product would not ship (a label cut at a fold, a column off its grid, a control the product does not have).
3. Coherence is measured first: one failed Q3 threshold (shadows over the cap, gaps under 95 % public / 90 % console) holds the scale at 3.5, two at 3; for a family, a second type voice or reading edge across its screens weighs as much as any per-screen number.
4. UX reads the family's own list literally — LA-9's five answers at three sizes for a console, RH-6's chain for a public page, the L-rows for an entrance — and a 4 tolerates only removable faults filed as conditions, never an answer missing from a first viewport.
5. Score only from stills you can name or from your own harness, and say what you read before scoring: the instruments themselves carry earlier verdicts on these three surfaces.
