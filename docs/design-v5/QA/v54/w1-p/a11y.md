## 0. In short

**What holds, measured:**
- **Keyboard order follows visual order.** On the landing it matches at 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 390 and 320, in fr and ar. On the index it matches at 1440 and 390.
- **Focus is always visible.** Every stop draws a 2 px `:focus-visible` ring at a 2 px offset, at 5.24–9.93 : 1 against what surrounds it.

- **The one-pair viewer works by keyboard.** It is modal, Tab stays inside, it closes on Escape or «Chiudi», it unloads to 0 frames, and focus returns to the link that opened it.
- **Every frame has a title.**
- **Text contrast passes everywhere.** The lowest text run is 5.29 : 1 on the index, out of 566 runs.
- **Reduced motion leaves nothing moving.**
- **Arabic pages render right-to-left inside their frames.**

- **Reflow holds at 320 × 256.**

**What fails:**
- **B1: a sticky strip hides the focus on the index.** At 390 px wide, Shift+Tab leaves five focused controls entirely under the strip. This fails WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.11 (AA). It also breaks plan §1.6 («Never hidden under a sticky bar»).

- **M2 (latent): the strip's quick links overflow the row.** They push the strip's own controls out of the window, and they are gone below 80 rem.

## 3. Findings

### Blocking

**B1. The index's sticky strip hides the focus.** This fails WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum), level AA, and plan §1.6.

- **Where.** `static/css/dev/designpreview.css:1193–1196` makes the strip `position: sticky` on `/__design-v5/index/`. Nothing reserves its 40 px when the browser scrolls a focused control into view. `html` has `scroll-padding-top: auto`. The only offset is `scroll-margin-block-start: 56px` on `.dp-index__section` (line 1861), which serves the anchors of the contents list, not the focus.
- **Measured.** E/04_obscured.json: Shift+Tab from the index's last control back to the strip, 76 steps.
  - **At 390 × 844, 5 focused controls end entirely under the strip:** «Entrer — Entreprise — Cantiere Adriatico», «fr / ar — Mes échéances» and «fr / ar — Profil». Each sits at y = 0 and is 32–36 px tall; the strip ends at 40. Four more are partly hidden, with 15–20 px of 32 showing. In E/04_index_focus_hidden_under_strip_390.png there is no ring anywhere on screen.
  - **At 1440 × 900, nothing is entirely hidden, but two folds nearly are.** The fold «V4 + fondation 33 pagine pubbliche» lands with 4 px of its 44 below the strip (E/03_index_focus_under_sticky_strip_1440.png). «Le chiavi delle superfici» shows 16 px.
  - The way in is ordinary: tab forward, then step back.
- **Fix.** In `designpreview.css`, give the index's scroller the strip's height:

  ```css
  .dp-live-root {
    block-size: 100%;
    -webkit-text-size-adjust: 100%;
    /* The index's strip is sticky (40 px): the focus and the anchors land below it (WCAG 2.4.11). */
    scroll-padding-block-start: 56px;
  }
  ```

  Then delete `scroll-margin-block-start: 56px` from `.dp-index__section` (line 1861). Otherwise the jumps from the contents list land 112 px down. The landing does not scroll at the root, so it is not affected.
- **Verified.** I injected the fix through CSSOM; the page's CSP allows no inline style. The same walk then gives **0 hidden and 0 partly hidden** at 390 and at 1440 (E/05_media.json, `fix_scroll_padding_56px`).
- **Test to add.** A browser test in `tests/frontend/test_design_preview.py`. At 390 × 844 and 1440 × 900, focus the index's last control, Shift+Tab back to the strip, and assert that no focused control's box intersects the strip's box.

### Major

**M1. Choosing the frame from «Altro» drops the focus to `<body>`.** This touches WCAG 2.4.3 and 2.4.7: the keyboard user loses their place and the ring disappears.

- **Where.** `static/js/dev/designpreview.js:218–221`. The script closes the `<details>` (`menu.open = false`) while the chip that was just activated, inside it, still has the focus. The chip disappears, and the focus falls to `<body>`.
- **Range.** From 48 rem to 80 rem: 768–1279 px windows, which is also a 1440 screen at 113–187 % zoom. In that range «Cornice» lives in «Altro». Below 48 rem it is not offered. From 80 rem the two links sit in the strip itself and keep the focus (measured: `C_wide_frame_toggle_1440`).
- **Measured.** E/02_menus.json, `B_altro_frame_toggle_1024`, at 1024 × 768. I pressed Tab to «Altro», then Enter, then Tab six times to «Telefono 390», then Enter.
  - The stage became the phone, and `aria-current` moved to both phone links, as it should.
  - `document.activeElement` became `BODY.dp-live`, and no ring is on screen (E/02_after_telefono_1024.png).
  - The next Tab goes into the frame.
- **Fix.** When the script closes a menu under the focus, give the focus back to that menu's summary:

  ```js
  var menu = link.closest("details");
  if (menu) {
    menu.open = false;
    menu.querySelector("summary").focus();
  }
  ```
- **Test to add.** A browser test at 1024 × 768. Open «Altro» by keyboard, press Enter on «Telefono 390», and assert that the focused element is the summary of «Altro» and that the stage has `dp-live__stage--phone`.

**M2. Latent: the strip's quick links push its controls out of the window, and vanish below 80 rem.**

- **Why it is latent.** The quick links are «the default path» of plan §4.1. They are drawn only when the steward's `live_status.json` names a surface `"v5"`. None does today, so nothing fails yet.
- **Simulated.** I injected the template's own markup (`_strip.html:33–35`) into the served page by request interception. No file and no database row were touched (E/08_quick_links.json).
  - **With 2 links:** at 1440, where the claim and the build show from 90 rem, «Apri da sola» is pushed 48 px past the window.
  - **With 4 links:** «Novità», «Indice» and «Apri da sola» are off-screen at 1440, by 230 px. «Indice» and «Apri da sola» are off-screen at 1536 (134 px) and at 1280 (95 px). «Personas» is cut (E/08_quick_links_4_1440.png).
  - The strip is a 40 px bar that does not wrap. Those controls stay invisible until the focus reaches them, and then the whole document scrolls sideways.
  - The simulation kept «V4 + fondation» in the state chip. «V5 — council-passed» is five characters longer, so the real overflow would be worse.
- **Below 80 rem.** The links carry `.dp-strip__wide` and so get `display: none`, and «Altro» does not repeat them (0 found). At 200 % zoom on a 1440 screen the default path is gone. That is a loss of function on zoom, against the spirit of WCAG 1.4.4 and 1.4.10. The index still lists the pages.
- **Why fix it now.** After W1-P, `apps/designpreview/**` has no writer (plan §2.2, the serialized files). The first Wave-2 merge writes the first `"v5"`.
- **Fix.** Draw the quick links as one more menu of the strip: a `<details class="dp-strip__menu">` «Superfici V5 · N», whose panel lists them, present at every width. The panel already behaves at 320 × 256. If they stay inline, at least repeat them in «Altro» and cap the inline ones to what the row can hold.
- **Then re-measure.** The row at 1280–1920, with 1, 4 and 8 surfaces passed, signed out and as `qa-company`, also under the text-spacing override (m4).
- **Tests to add.**
  - A client test with `override_settings(ILE_DESIGN_PREVIEW_LIVE_STATUS=<a temporary file with N surfaces "v5">)`, asserting that the links sit in a menu that is present below 80 rem.
  - A browser check that `document.documentElement.scrollWidth == innerWidth` at 1280 and 1440.

### Minor

**m1. The landing's frame title becomes a path, and the window title never follows the frame.**

- **Where.** `landing.html:32` serves a good title, «La home — V4 + fondation». It is pinned by `test_the_landing_frames_the_live_home_once_under_the_strip`. But `designpreview.js:152` (`frame.title = page + " — " + label`) runs on every load of the frame, the first one included.
- **Effect.** A screen reader meets «/fr/ — V4 + fondation» or «/fr/espace-reserve/ — V4 + fondation». All 8 landing loads measured ended with the path (E/01_landing_tab.json, E/06_persona.json). The window's `<title>` stays «V5 dal vivo · Italy Legal Entry™» whatever the frame holds.
- **Fix.** The view already computes the titles. Put them in the map: `"titles": titles` beside `"states"` in `live.strip()`, from `_known_paths`. Then in `follow()`:

  ```js
  var name = map.titles[path] || (frame.contentDocument && frame.contentDocument.title) || page;
  frame.title = name + " — " + label;
  document.title = name + " · V5 dal vivo · Italy Legal Entry™";
  ```
- **Test to add.** A browser test: after load, the frame's title is still «La home — V4 + fondation»; after a navigation inside the frame, it names that page.

**m2. A strip menu stays open when the focus leaves it, and Escape then pulls the focus back.**

- **Where it happens.** On the index. The landing has no such case: there, the menus close when the focus enters the frame.
- **Measured.** The open «Personas» panel stays over the page while the focus moves on into the page (E/11_panel_widths.json, E/03_index.json).
  - At 1024–1920 it covers corners of the focused contents links «Stato delle famiglie», «Fondazione», «Confronto con la V4» and «Archivio della ricerca».
  - It never covers one entirely, so 2.4.11 holds. 2.4.12 (AAA) does not.
  - Pressing Escape then, with the focus in the page, closes the panel but sends the focus back to «Personas» (`designpreview.js:91–97`), and the user's place is lost.
- **Fix.** In part 2 of the script, close a menu when the keyboard leaves it, and let Escape move the focus only if it was inside:

  ```js
  menu.addEventListener("focusout", function (event) {
    if (menu.open && event.relatedTarget && !menu.contains(event.relatedTarget)) {
      menu.open = false;
    }
  });
  /* the Escape handler */
  var open = event.key === "Escape" ? strip.querySelector("details[open]") : null;
  if (open) {
    var inside = open.contains(document.activeElement);
    open.open = false;
    if (inside) {
      open.querySelector("summary").focus();
    }
  }
  ```

  `relatedTarget` is null when someone clicks the panel's text, so reading a panel with the mouse does not close it.

**m3. Forced colours erase the persona dot and the «current» tiles.**

- **Measured.** Under emulated `forced-colors: active`, backgrounds become Canvas (E/05_forced_colors_*.png, E/06_persona_in_strip_390_forced_colors.png).
  - **The persona dot disappears.** It is `rgb(255,255,255)` on a `rgb(255,255,255)` strip. Below 48 rem, where the persona's name is visually hidden, the dot is the only visible sign that a persona is in, so nothing says it.
  - **The state chip loses its dot.**
  - **The current language and frame lose their tile.** They differ from the others only by weight, 500 → 600.
  - Outside forced colours, the current tile is mid-3 on mid-1, at 1.30 : 1. Its real cues are the weight and a lighter text.
- **Fix.**

  ```css
  @media (forced-colors: active) {
    .dp-strip__dot,
    .dp-strip__state::before,
    .dp-state::before {
      forced-color-adjust: none;
      background: CanvasText;
    }

    .dp-strip__link[aria-current="true"],
    .dp-strip__chip[aria-current="true"] {
      text-decoration: underline 2px;
      text-underline-offset: 6px;
    }
  }
  ```

  Outside forced colours, consider a 2 px `--accent-dark` bar under the current item, so it does not rest on a 1.30 : 1 tile. That one is for the design reviewers to decide.

**m4. Text spacing (WCAG 1.4.12): the one-row strip overflows, and its two `<p>` labels ride up.**

- **Measured.** I applied the 1.4.12 override through CSSOM: line-height 1.5, letter-spacing 0.12 em, word-spacing 0.16 em, paragraph spacing 2 em (E/05_media.json `text_spacing`).
  - At 1440, «Apri da sola» runs 65 px past the window, and the document becomes 1 505 px wide.
  - At 320, «Altro» runs 20 px past.
  - At every width, the state chip and the build, which are `<p>` elements, shift up by half their new paragraph margin and are clipped at the top edge (E/05_text_spacing_landing_{1280,390}.png).
- **Fix.**
  - Make the build and the state `<span>` elements (`_strip.html:25–26`). They are labels in a bar, not paragraphs. The script finds the state through `[data-dp-state]`, which does not change.
  - Give the row the headroom the override needs. For example, show the claim and the build from 100 rem instead of 90, or move the build into «Novità».
  - Re-measure the strip at 320–1920 with the override, as the r5 series did without it.

**m5. The viewer's toggles cannot be released.**

- **Where.** «Desktop 1440» and «Telefono 390» carry `aria-pressed` (`designpreview.js:252–260`).
- **Effect.** Pressing one that is already pressed does nothing; both stay `true` (E/03_index.json `desktop_pressed_again`). A screen reader announces a toggle that does not toggle.
- **Fix.** Either let the second press unload that frame (first `about:blank`, then remove it, as `unload()` does for the pair), which makes it a true toggle within the one-pair cap, or drop `aria-pressed`, set `aria-disabled="true"`, and change the text to «Desktop 1440 — aperta».

**m6. French and Arabic words carry no `lang` in the Italian pages** (WCAG 3.1.2 Language of Parts).

- **Measured.** The only `lang` under `<html lang="it">` is the logo lockup's `en`.
- **What is affected.**
  - «Entrer» and «Sortir».
  - The persona names.
  - The persona pages: «Tableau de bord», «Mes pièces», «Mon parcours» and the rest.
  - «fondation».
  - The language tooltips «Français», «العربية» and «English».
- **Effect.** A screen reader reads them all with the Italian voice.
- **Why it is only minor.** Most of these are proper names in the plan, and each fix is one attribute.
- **Fix.**
  - Add `lang="fr"` on `.dp-persona__name`, `.dp-persona__button`, `.dp-card__name`, `.dp-card__button`, `.dp-card__title` and `.dp-strip__who`.
  - Name the languages in Italian in the tooltips: `LANG_NAMES` (`live.py:73`) becomes «Francese», «Arabo», «Italiano», «Inglese».

**m7. Some information lives only in a `title`, and 80 links open a new tab without saying so.**

- **Title-only information.**
  - The meaning of the state chip, «Lo stato della pagina nella cornice», is a `title` on a `<p>`.
  - The language links are named «fr», «ar», «it» and «en»; the language itself is only in the `title`.
  - «Apri da sola» says «in una nuova scheda» only in its `title`.
- **Silent new tabs.** The `target="_blank"` links say nothing: 2 on the landing and 78 on the index. They are each «V4 · 8771», the states, the error pages, «La home V4», and the viewer's «Apri la pagina da sola».
- **Why it matters.** A `title` reaches neither keyboard nor touch users, and screen readers read it unreliably.
- **Fix,** using the existing `.dp-hidden` utility:
  - **State chip.** Put `<span class="dp-hidden">Stato della pagina nella cornice: </span>` before the label, and hold the label in its own span. `follow()` sets the chip's `textContent`, so it must then set that span instead.
  - **Language links.** Write `fr<span class="dp-hidden"> — francese</span>`, and the same for the others.
  - **New tabs.** Put `<span class="dp-hidden"> (nuova scheda)</span>` inside every `target="_blank"` link.

**m8. The index's folds do not look like folds.**

- **Where.** `.dp-index__summary` is `display: flex` (line 1886). That removes the native disclosure marker, and the sheet draws none in its place.
- **Effect.** «V4 + fondation · 33 pagine pubbliche» and «Le chiavi delle superfici, per lo steward» give no visual sign that they open, or whether they are open; only a hairline appears when open (E/03_index_desktop_link_focused_1440.png). Chrome exposes them correctly as disclosure triangles with `expanded`, so the gap is visual only.
- **Fix.** Give them the strip's chevron. Add `.dp-index__summary::after` with the same rules as `.dp-strip__summary::after` plus `margin-inline-start: auto`, and rotate it under `.dp-index__fold[open] > .dp-index__summary::after`. Put its transition inside the existing `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` block.

**m9. The strip's persona list does not mark the persona who is in.**

- **Measured.** While a persona is in, its row still offers «Entrer — ‹persona›». Only the name is bolder, and that is a CSS weight, not something a screen reader hears. The summary's accessible name reads «Personas , dentro come …», with a stray space that comes from the flex items.
- **Fix.**
  - In `_personas.html`, give the current persona «attiva» in place of the button: `{% if persona.key == strip.persona_key %}<span class="dp-persona__off">attiva</span>{% elif persona.allowed %}…`.
  - Write the hidden text as « — dentro come ».

**Overlap with the UX review.** I read `ux.md`, written in parallel, only after measuring. My M2 is its M5 (the quick
links), and my m8 is its M4 (the folds). We measured both independently and they agree.

## 4. Observations (no finding)

- **O1. Escape does not close the viewer while the focus is inside one of its frames.** This is how browsers behave: the key goes to the framed document. One Shift+Tab from the frame's first control reaches «Chiudi» (E/04_obscured.json).

When W1-A lands `--focus-ring` and `--focus-ring-night` (§1.6), `var(--focus-ring-night, var(--accent-dark))` and `var(--focus-ring, var(--accent))` would let the preview chrome follow the V5 focus system without an edit.
- **O4. The read-only refusal describes the wrong page.** It is `text/plain` with no language, which is fine for a development sentence. But after it, the strip and the frame's title still describe the page that was posted to, not the refusal.
- **O5. The archive's pickers keep their single-character shortcuts.** They predate V5.4 and moved as they were; 2.1.4 would want a way to turn them off. They are off the main path and not W1-P's change.
